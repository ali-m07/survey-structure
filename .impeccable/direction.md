No false metrics, testimonials, pricing or fabricated team biographies. Browser access controls complement existing server authorization.

## Continuous depth motion and localization

The user requests automatic, continuously playing 3D movement, not pointer-only tilt. The focal sequence is a survey sheet lifting out of the stack, moving toward the viewer, then settling back while the underlying logic and report sheets separate and return. A 7-second seamless cycle changes actual perspective depth and rotation. Pointer tilt composes on the parent. Controls acknowledge selection in 150ms; focus remains stable. A visible pause/play control and system reduced-motion preference keep the demonstration usable. No section is hidden pending scroll animation.

Three locale routes use i18next/react-i18next resources and Next.js locale routing, with Persian RTL and English/French LTR. Layout expands for French strings, keeps editorial reading measure, and provides a stable public header and language switcher. User survey content is not translated automatically.
