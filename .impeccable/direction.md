User brief: complete Persian website, introduction, about, three-dimensional site and login; permissions determined after generic login.
Brand: Porsnama / پرس‌نما, coined for this project at the user's request (availability not verified).

Grounded systems considered: research journal, survey branching map, respondent conversation, dimensional research desk, data exhibition, typography poster, fieldwork notebook. Seed f6469b06 assigned fourth: dimensional research desk. Remote catalog unavailable after escalated retry; no challenger boards were available. Code-led build using CSS 3D product geometry, no raster comp.

Contract: cream paper surface, deep plum ink, lavender dimensional questionnaire sheets, self-hosted Vazirmatn. Large editorial Persian heading and real product preview occupy first viewport. Primary action is generic login; visitor can try a labeled anonymous sample before authentication. Three-dimensional sheets respond to pointer; keyboard sample and reduced motion remain usable. Navigation reaches product, about and login; footer provides truthful product identity without invented contacts or company claims. Login inherits depth scene and visual system. Server-returned organization memberships govern workspace and controls; no role selection at login.
Quality bar: readable sample questions, purposeful perspective and lighting rather than arbitrary shapes; phone viewport must retain copy and a compact interactive sample. No false metrics, testimonials, pricing or fabricated team biographies. Browser access controls complement existing server authorization.
